Robotics 3

The first course in the elementary robotics sequence.

Grades: 3
Length: 4 Sessions
Teacher: Mr. Cooper

In Robotics 3 is the introductory class to the elementary school robotics program. Students will use Edison Robotics, which are an age appropriate and challenging robotics platform that allows students to learn about computational thinking. Students will learn how to communicate with the robots and the importance of being detailed when giving sets of instructions.

  • Meeting 1: Precise Instructions Unplugged Activity
  • Meeting 2: Card Coding with Edison & Ed Sumo
  • Meeting 3: Movement & Maze
  • Meeting 4: Distance Sensor & Obstacle Course
