Our Equipment & Tools

From precision digital fabrication to classic shop tools, these are the machines students use to learn design, build prototypes and test ideas safely and professionally.

Digital Fabrication CNC & Milling Woodworking Hand Tools Electronics Safety

Digital Fabrication

Prusa 3-D Print Farm

Prusa MK3's are reliable 3-D printers that run around the clock to print student parts. These printers use Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) with PLA, and we also use ABS when extra strength is needed.

  • Build volume: ~256 × 256 × 256 mm
  • Nozzle: 0.4 mm
  • Materials: PLA, ABS (plus others)
Digital FabAdditive

3-D Printers: Bambu Labs X1 Carbon

This printer is ~50% faster than most traditional FDM printers. It features an enclosed chamber for even heating and a material system that allows for multi-color prints.

  • Build volume: ~256 × 256 × 256 mm
  • Nozzle: 0.4 mm
  • Materials: PLA, ABS (plus others)
Digital FabAdditive

3-D Printers: Peopoly SLA Printer

SLA printing for ultimate precision. Uses UV-curable resin and is ideal for extremely small, highly detailed parts.

  • Build volume: ~13.6 × 17.6 × 15.7 in
  • Materials: UV resin
Digital FabAdditive

Lasecutter: Universal

High-speed, precision lasercutter that uses a concentrated laser beam to cut, score and etch a variety of materials.

  • Working Area: 18" x 30"
  • Power: 45W
Digital FabAdditive

Lasecutter: Epilog Mini

A smaller high-speed, precision lasercutter that uses a concentrated laser beam to cut, score and etch a variety of materials.

  • Working Area: 12" x 24"
  • Power: 40W
Digital FabAdditive

Lasecutter: Glowforge

A students friendly lasercutter that allows for jobs to be sent remotely to the laser.

  • Working Area: 11" x 20"
  • Power: 40W
Digital FabAdditive

Computer Numeric Control (CNC) & Milling

CNC: Tormach PCNC 770 S3

Precision Computer Numeric Controlled milling for plastics and soft metals. CNC machines allow students to create 3D models and manufacture them out of a variety of materials including metal. CNC milling is referred to as "subtractive manufacturing" vs. an "additive process" like 3-D printing."

Woodshop

Saw Stop Tablesaw

SawStop is an advanced safety technology that uses a retractable blade system designed to instantly stop and drop the blade upon detecting contact with human skin, preventing serious injury. Table saws are used to make straight repeatable cuts in materials.

  • SawStop Safety System
  • Throat: 20in
Woodshop

Spindle Sanders

Spindle sanders are used to quickly and efficiently sand wood, create edges and smooth holes and concave edges.

Electronics

Arduino Microcontrollers

Arduino microcontrollers are open source platforms designed for rapid prototyping in hardware and electronics. The Arduino Uno R3 is commonly used for general projects, while the Arduino Micro is ideal when a smaller footprint is required.

  • Open-source and versatile for quick prototyping.
  • Available in Uno R3 and compact Micro versions.
  • Works with many sensors and add-on shields.
Electronics

Sensors

Various sensors can be integrated with Arduino and other electronics to enhance projects with additional functionality. These sensors can measure a wide range of data, including temperature, distance, and moisture.

  • Detects temperature, distance, and moisture.
  • Includes analog and digital sensor types.
  • Easily connects to Arduino for real-world data.
