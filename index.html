Jericho Technology Program

At Jericho UFSD students explore engineering, computer science, robotics, woodworking and design, through project based learning. From laser-cut instruments and 3D-printed prototypes to coding, electronics and iterative product design, our courses emphasize creativity and problem-solving. Real-world technical skills are learned throughout all of the labs.
